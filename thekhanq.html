Our Story

THE KHANQ reimagines Turkic culinary heritage through modern techniques — a tasting journey designed to celebrate seasonality, technique and ceremony. Each menu tells a story, each plate is a chapter.

Craft & Philosophy

We source ingredients from Central Asian agro-communities, combine fermentation and smoke with modern plating, and aim to create sensory narratives that stay with guests long after dessert.
